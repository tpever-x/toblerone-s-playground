The sky above this CV is real.

No screenshot, no looped video. The sun position is solved from your OS clock and coordinates with spherical trigonometry; the gradient, the droplet caustics, the aurora and the water reflections all fall out of that one calculation. Precipitation comes from a live Open-Meteo feed. Since v3.0 the atmosphere is ray-marched on your GPU through a multi-pass WebGL2 pipeline — bloom, scattering, 250,000 weather particles and an N-body arena, all running while you read this sentence. I treat the browser the way I treat a circuit — every frame is a clock cycle, and nothing here is faked.
